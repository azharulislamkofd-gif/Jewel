How it works: the renderer keeps the full-body portrait sharp in the centre of a
16:9 stage built from de-focused edges of the same still, then adds a slow
orbital parallax + push-in, panning cyan/gold spotlight beams, drifting fractal
smoke, flickering side sparks, beat-pulsed crowd glows and a gentle localised
warp for hair / skirt-coat / stance. It is a procedural animation of one still,
not a generative video model, so no new bodily motion or audio is synthesised.
The 4K file is a Lanczos upscale of the 1080p master; fine detail is limited by
the 843 px wide source still.
